Sophie Grimme

Sophie Grimme is a Postdoc at the IT University of Copenhagen working on machine learning competitions and how to make them more diverse, inclusive and sustainable. Her background is in Human Computer Interaction and in her PhD she focused on digital technologies for women's health and how to design them in a way that is enhancing data authority.
